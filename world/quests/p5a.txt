# Side-quest chain A (PKM-75, design PKM-51): the Linden Field Notes. Volume N is quest step N-1 and unlocks with N+1 badges.

quest p5a_linden_notes "The Linden Field Notes"
  desc "Prof. Linden studies how people and Pokémon live together. He mails field notes, one volume per badge pair, and asks you to observe what he cannot."
  step "Vol. 1, Bond observation: show Linden's contact in Brindle City's Pokémon Center a bonded starter."
  step "Vol. 2, Night sighting: photograph a Joltik at night with the trail camera on Route 7, then report to Mira's lab in Voltara City."
  step "Vol. 3, Fossil lineage: revive a fossil in Quarrytown and show it to Linden's contact in Lumen Village."
  step "Vol. 4, Mist behaviour: take Mistwood fog readings in the morning, by day and at night, then report to the Fairy Keeper in Seraphine Town."
  step "Vol. 5, Thermal study: set three sensors around Ashfall by day, read them after dark and report to the Magma Elder's house."
  step "Vol. 6, Cold-weather behaviour: log all three markers along Route 12 and report to the house in Frostmere Town."
  step "Vol. 7, Final thesis: defend the thesis at the Collector's House in Dragonspire City."
end
