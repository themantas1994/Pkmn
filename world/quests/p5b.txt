# Side-quest chain B (PKM-77, design PKM-51): Kai's Notebook. Step N is the entry the player is working on; the last step completes the quest.

quest p5b_kai_notebook "Kai's Notebook"
  desc "Your rival left torn pages of his notebook across Tessera. Each one points to the next town, and to what he was thinking when he wrote it."
  step "Entry 1: a page says Kai hid a cache on Route 3, behind a Cut tree in the far west corner."
  step "Entry 2: win over the three judges of the Port Tidewater cooking contest (badge 2)."
  step "Entry 3: put the glitching terminals in the Voltara Mira Lab back in the right order (badge 3)."
  step "Entry 4: reset the three mine-cart rails in Quarrytown in the order the carts run (badge 4)."
  step "Entry 5: spar with Kai in Seraphine Town (badge 5)."
  step "Entry 6: find Kai in Frostmere Town and read his apology (badge 7)."
  step "Capstone: meet Kai in the Victory Road side room (badge 8)."
end
